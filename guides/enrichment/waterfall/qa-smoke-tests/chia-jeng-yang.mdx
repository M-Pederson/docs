**Read the Δ column with one caveat first.** The sandbox graph is *warm* — it accumulated Cambridge, Harvard Business School and the rest from earlier, unrelated runs. The universe column is one run against a DB wiped for this person. A person run does not enrich its fan-out inline; it parks it in `universe.enrichment_queue`, and the drain only picks `priority_tier <= 1` (Xano's Task #127 rule, ported). After this run 2 of 17 queued rows were eligible and were drained; the other 15 — Cambridge, Harvard Business School, Anglo-Chinese School, SCDF, AWS, Udemy, e27, On Deck… — sat at tier 4 with count 1–2 and **would not have drained in production either**. Company rows promote one tier per 3 enqueues (#12855), so those become work only once other people point at them.

So the location- and school-shaped part of the gap measures graph age, not pipeline capability.

Known causes for the gaps, tracked in `internal/features/enrichment/person/TODO.md`:

- **HAS_EXPERTISE 5 vs 11** — the taxonomy matcher resolves everything it is given; PDL's 27 skills never reach the prompt. `HAS_SUBDOMAIN` is ours alone, the expertise taxonomy backbone.
- **Issuer edges half-resolved** — "SCDF" and "Homerton College, Cambridge" are an abbreviation and a rewording of orgs held under other names; the 10 unmatched rows sit in `curation.human_review_queue` as `credential_org_unresolvable`.

Chia's payload holds `accomplishment_publications: 5` (the e27 / Varsity / Wilberforce / Concord Review pieces) and `articles: 4` (LinkedIn Pulse posts) — we wrote exactly 5, and 5 + 4 is the sandbox's 9. `elPublications` now emits both families; articles carry no publisher, so they get `AUTHORED` without `PUBLISHED_BY`.

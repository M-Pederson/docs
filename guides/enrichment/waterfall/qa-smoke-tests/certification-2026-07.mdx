## How the campaign worked

Each fixture ran the same loop: **certified wipe → seed → settle → fail-closed gate**.
A gate failure was treated as the system reporting a latent defect; the defect was
root-caused (crash rows → receipts → function request history), fixed with a
byte-verified live publish, and the fixture re-run from a fresh wipe. No fixture was
passed by loosening its gate; two gates were *added* (the parked-person contract, and
LLM tense adjudication below) where the existing gate measured the wrong contract.

Roughly 40 defects/fixes shipped across the campaign — classification-vs-crash
discipline (telemetry rows carry no `error_message`), data-loss guards, identity
dedup, provider resilience, and grammar-validator hardening. Per-fixture defect
ledgers live on the fixture pages above.

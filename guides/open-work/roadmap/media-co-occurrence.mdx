**Status: scope & planning only.** Nothing on this page is built. Goal: extend the podcast ingestion lane (curated media/tech/VC podcast cron: guest \+ LinkedIn resolution, hosts connected to guests in the graph) into a general speaking/media co-occurrence waterfall covering **YouTube interviews**, **conference speaker rosters**, and **GDELT news co-mentions**. All three are free sources; they differ sharply in signal strength and identity risk, so each gets its own gates.

## Entry points

Same invariant as the stage-production lane: every route enqueues **appearances or events** (or person-lookups that resolve to them), and all mentions flow through the shared extraction → resolution → edge-writer stages. GDELT is the one exception: it never creates people or appearance nodes, only aggregated edges between already-resolved graph persons (see gates below).

| # | Entry point | Direction | Mechanism | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Curated roster seeding** | Source → people | The podcast-set pattern extended: a config roster of interview YouTube channels and ICP conferences, seeded once (historical backfill), then polled. | P1 |
| 2 | **Public-engagement persona trigger** | Person → appearances | A person whose persona narratives or enrichment signals show public speaking / media presence → person-side lookup across YouTube search and conference agenda archives, hits enqueue the source events. | P0 |
| 3 | **Podcast-guest crossover** | Person → appearances | Everyone already resolved as a podcast guest gets a one-time YouTube \+ conference lookup; the populations overlap heavily. | P0 |
| 4 | **Discover misses** | Person → appearances | Discover Deep Research resolves someone as a media-visible operator → approved miss enters via the person-side lookup. | P0 |
| 5 | **Recurring crons** | Source → people | Channel-upload polling (YouTube), conference-season sweeps against the roster, GDELT batch pulls. Cadence per source below. | P1 |
| 6 | **Roster expansion** | Source → source | Channels and conferences that keep surfacing already-tracked people get promoted into the roster (agent-suggested, human-approved), the self-learning intelligence-layer pattern. | P2 |

## Decisions (2026-08-21)

| Question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| GDELT scoring | **Evidence-first.** `CO_MENTIONED_WITH` ships as intelligence-layer evidence only in v1: visible in narratives and layers, excluded from leverage-loop and outcome scoring. Promotion to scored edges is a later call made against observed precision. |
| Co-mention floor & decay | ≥3 distinct articles across ≥2 source domains, counted over a rolling 24-month window; stale pairs age out. |
| Promotion criteria | Random 100-pair manual audit after 60 days of collection; promote into scoring only at ≥90% validated precision, quarterly re-audits thereafter. |
| Scored levels | Interview host↔guest and co-panelists score against §3.6 bands. Conference-level co-occurrence is evidence-only, like GDELT. |
| Captions | Metadata-only extraction in v1. Phase 0 measures the miss rate; caption fallback ships later only if the gap justifies it. |
| Roster governance | Agent suggestions land as `proposed` rows in `media_source_roster`; founder approves from a review queue, the merge-suspects pattern. |
| Historical windows | Full channel history for roster YouTube channels; GDELT limited to the rolling 24-month window, no 2015 backfill. |
| §3.6 bands | Set 2026-08-21 in the cross-lane sitting: `W_interview` 15, `W_panel` 20; traversal weights on unbanded edges: conference `APPEARED_IN` 45, `PART_OF` 30, `CO_MENTIONED_WITH` 55. |

## Why

Who appears with whom is the public tier of the relationship graph. An interview is an hour of documented one-on-one contact between host and guest; a panel is curated peer grouping by a third party; repeated news co-mention is market-perceived association. All three are visible for exactly the ICP: operators in finance, real estate, media, and tech who speak. The podcast lane already proves the mechanics (episode → guest extraction → LinkedIn resolution → host-guest edges); this plan generalizes the mechanics across three more surfaces without changing the write path.

Signal ordering, strongest to weakest: interview (host↔guest, 1:1) \> panel co-panelists (same session) \> co-speakers (same conference) \> news co-mention (same article). Weights and gates follow that ordering.

## The key structural fact

The three sources sit at opposite ends of the identity-risk spectrum:

- **YouTube and conferences are appearance-anchored**: a video ID or a session on an agenda is a concrete event with names attached in context (title, description, bio blurb, employer line). Identity resolution has corroborating material, the same shape as podcast episodes.
- **GDELT is string-anchored**: the GKG `V2Persons` field is a bare list of person-name strings per article, no context, massive volume, and name collisions everywhere. It is useless for discovering people and dangerous for auto-linking.

So the design splits: YouTube/conferences extend the podcast pipeline as new source adapters with full Stage A/B/C treatment. GDELT runs as a **closed-world overlay**: match article person strings only against names already resolved in the graph, apply a name-uniqueness gate, and write only aggregated co-mention edges with count thresholds. GDELT can strengthen edges; it can never mint identities.

<Tip>
  **Quota economics decide the YouTube design.** Data API v3 gives ~10k units/day; `search.list` costs 100 units, `playlistItems.list` costs 1. Enumerating a curated channel roster's uploads is ~free; global search is 100x more expensive and noisy. Roster-first, search only for person-side lookups (#2/#3), metadata-only extraction in v1. Verify quota math in Phase 0.
</Tip>

## Node & edge schemas (new in this scope)

This lane introduces **two node types** and **three edge types** to the ontology. Both node types are shared with the podcast lane: one definition, and whichever lane ships first registers them. Everything else (Person, Company) is existing.

### Node: `MediaAppearance` (new)

| Property | Type | Req | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `uuid` | string | ✓ | Envelope. |
| `source` | enum `podcast` \| `youtube` \| `conference_session` | ✓ | Discriminator. |
| `title` | string | ✓ | Episode/video/session title as published. |
| `valid_from` | date | ✓ | Publish or session date, bitemporal convention. |
| `video_id` | string |  | YouTube anchor. |
| `episode_ref` | string |  | Podcast anchor; simulcast dedup collapses audio \+ video onto one node carrying both refs. |
| `session_ref` | string |  | Conference agenda anchor. |
| `via_roster` | string |  | `media_source_roster` entry that produced it. |
| `source_urls` | string\[\] | ✓ | Provenance. |
| `confidence` | enum | ✓ | From extraction. |
| envelope |  | ✓ | `created_at`, `updated_at`, `weight`, deterministic `description` ("Episode of The TED AI Show" / "Session at TED2026"), `description_generated_at`, per the 2026-07-04 standard. |

### Edge: `APPEARED_IN` (new): `(Person)-[:APPEARED_IN]->(MediaAppearance)`

| Property | Type | Req | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `role` | enum `host` \| `guest` \| `panelist` \| `speaker` | ✓ | Drives pair derivation. |
| `weight` | float | ✓ | Per the band table below. |
| `mention_ref` | id | ✓ | Audit link to `media_appearance_mention`. |
| `source_urls`, `confidence` |  | ✓ | Provenance \+ resolution outcome. |
| envelope |  | ✓ | Deterministic description: "Bilawal Sidhu appeared in \<title\> as host". |

Idempotent per (person, appearance).

### Edge: `PART_OF` (new): `(MediaAppearance)-[:PART_OF]->(Event)`

Structural containment: traversal weight **30** (the `RECORDING_OF` analogue), no band. Exists so session-level and conference-level co-occurrence stay distinguishable at query time.

### Edge: `CO_MENTIONED_WITH` (new): `(Person)-[:CO_MENTIONED_WITH]->(Person)`

| Property | Type | Req | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `article_count` | int | ✓ | Distinct articles in window. |
| `window` | string | ✓ | `24mo` rolling. |
| `window_counts` | json | ✓ | Per-quarter breakdown for decay visibility. |
| `first_seen` / `last_seen` | date | ✓ |  |
| `top_source_domains` | string\[\] | ✓ | Floor requires ≥2. |
| `evidence_refs` | id\[\] | ✓ | Rows in `news_comention_rollup`; article detail stays relational. |
| `layer` | `evidence` | ✓ | Per the evidence-first decision. |
| `excluded_from_scoring` | bool `true` | ✓ | Hard flag scoring respects. |
| envelope |  | ✓ | Deterministic description: "X and Y co-appear in news coverage". |

Written once per pair (canonical uuid ordering), updated by the daily batch, retired when the rolling window drops the pair below floor.

### Weight bands (set 2026-08-21, cross-lane §3.6 sitting)

| Relationship class | Derivation | Weight / pair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interview host↔guest | Two `APPEARED_IN` on one interview appearance | **15** per edge, pair 30 (`COLLABORATORS`-class; episode nodes are degree-3, no hub risk) |
| Co-panelists, same session | Two `APPEARED_IN {role: panelist}` on one session | **20** per edge, pair 40 (`CO_NOMINATED_WITH`-grade) |
| Co-speakers, same conference | Shared `Event` via `PART_OF` | No band: evidence-only. Conference-speaker `APPEARED_IN` writes traversal weight **45** (the `PERFORMED_AT` analogue). |
| News co-mention | `CO_MENTIONED_WITH` | No band: evidence-only. Traversal weight **55**. |

### Context richness (v1.1 candidates)

- Episode/session summaries stored on the appearance node and vectorized: direct evidence feeding the public-engagement persona narrative.
- Topic tagging per appearance against the 70-domain expertise taxonomy: semantic scoring learns what they talked about, not just that they talked.
- Employer capture from descriptions and bios into mention `identity_points`, corroborating Person→Company affiliations at zero extra fetch cost.

## Pipeline design per source

Go on GCP, per-market/source adapters behind one fetcher interface, raw payloads cached to GCS, Pub/Sub → Cloud Run, mentions in AlloyDB, edges to FalkorDB.

### YouTube adapter

1. Roster poll: uploads playlist per channel (1 unit/call), new video IDs → queue.
2. Guest extraction from title \+ description with the #12984 tier-1 config. Metadata-only in v1 per the decision above; Phase 0 measures the residual miss rate, and caption fallback ships later only if the gap justifies it. 2a. Podcast dedup: match against podcast-lane episodes by title \+ date \+ host so simulcasts strengthen one appearance instead of minting two.
3. Resolution: the podcast lane's LinkedIn resolution flow, plus #12677 v5 adjudication for names without a URL. Auto-link ≥0.85; name \+ two corroborating points minimum; #13168 cap applies.
4. Edge writer: `APPEARED_IN` with role (host/guest/panelist), idempotent per person-video pair.

### Conference adapter

1. Per-event adapter against the roster; current-year agenda live, past years via Wayback CDX.
2. Deterministic parse of speaker and session pages; #12984 tier-1 only for unstructured bio blurbs. Bio title \+ employer are the identity points.
3. Resolution as above; conference bios resolve unusually well (employer stated in-source).
4. Edge writer: session `MediaAppearance` \+ `PART_OF` event; co-panelist derivation comes free.

### GDELT overlay (closed-world)

1. Daily BigQuery batch: GKG rows where ≥2 `V2Persons` strings match the tracked-name index. Backfill covers the rolling 24-month window only; no 2015 deep backfill.
2. **Uniqueness gate**: a tracked person's name enters the matchable index only above a name-uniqueness score (corpus rarity \+ no same-name collision inside the graph). Common names never match, regardless of volume.
3. **Corroboration boost**: `V2Organizations` containing the person's known employer upgrades match confidence; tone and theme fields stored, not gated on.
4. Aggregation: pair counts rolled up over the rolling 24-month window; `CO_MENTIONED_WITH` written/updated only above the decided floor (≥3 distinct articles, ≥2 distinct source domains), and pairs that fall below it as the window rolls get retired. Evidence article refs stay relational.
5. Never creates persons, appearances, or queue rows. Overlay only.

## Draft system prompt v0.1: video guest extraction (not deployed)

```text
You extract interview participants from ONE YouTube video's metadata.

Channel: {{channel_name}} (known hosts: {{known_hosts}})
Title: {{title}}
Description: {{description}}

Return ONLY valid JSON: {"participants": [...]}. Each participant object:
- name: exactly as written
- role: "host" | "guest" | "panelist" | "unknown"
- identity_points: {title, employer, linkedin_url, twitter_handle, other} (nulls allowed)
- evidence_quote: verbatim fragment naming this participant
- confidence: "high" | "medium" | "low"

Rules:
1. Known channel hosts default to role "host" unless the metadata contradicts it.
2. Names mentioned as topics, not participants ("discussing Elon Musk's latest…"), are excluded.
3. Extract LinkedIn/Twitter URLs and stated titles/employers from the description into identity_points.
4. Multi-guest panels: every named participant gets its own object with role "panelist".
5. If no participants are identifiable, return {"participants": []}.
```

Adjudication follows #12677 v5, drafted against real cases in the LLM Test Harness before deployment.

## Worked example: Bilawal Sidhu (simulated 2026-08-21)

Dry run against public data; nothing written. Subject: TED tech curator and TED2026 co-host, inaugural host of The TED AI Show (38 episodes), a16z scout, creator with 2.1M\+ subscribers across a dual-brand channel structure. A deliberately hard case: host role, guest role, conference speaker, dual channels, and heavy news presence in one person.

**Entry points fired:** #2 public-engagement persona, #3 podcast-guest crossover, and #6 in reverse: his professional channel and The TED AI Show land in `media_source_roster` as `proposed` for approval.

**Extraction notes:** the entertainment channel (billyfx: VFX/AI content, ~990K TikTok, ~400K YouTube) returns empty participant sets at tier-1 cost and is skipped, validating channel-level rosters. Simulcast dedup collapses each TED AI Show episode's podcast audio and YouTube video onto one `MediaAppearance` carrying both anchors.

```text
// Interview pair (scored, W_interview)
(:MediaAppearance {source: "podcast", title: "The TED AI Show — Eric Schmidt",
  episode_ref, video_id, valid_from, via_roster: "ted-ai-show",
  description: "Episode of The TED AI Show"})
(bilawal:Person)-[:APPEARED_IN {role: "host", weight: W_interview}]->(ep)
(schmidt:Person)-[:APPEARED_IN {role: "guest", weight: W_interview}]->(ep)

// Conference session + containment (co-panel scored, W_panel)
(demo:MediaAppearance {source: "conference_session", session_ref})
      -[:PART_OF]->(ted2026:Event {name: "TED2026", start_date, venue})
(bilawal)-[:APPEARED_IN {role: "panelist", weight: W_panel}]->(demo)
(wickert:Person)-[:APPEARED_IN {role: "panelist", weight: W_panel}]->(demo)

// GDELT overlay (evidence layer, never scored)
(bilawal)-[:CO_MENTIONED_WITH {article_count: 14, window: "24mo",
  first_seen, last_seen, top_source_domains, evidence_refs,
  layer: "evidence", excluded_from_scoring: true}]->(schmidt)
```

## Rollout order

1. **Phase 0 spike (3-4 days)**: YouTube: 10-channel roster, quota math, measure what fraction of videos yield guests from metadata alone. Conferences: 2 event adapters incl. one Wayback backfill, bio-resolution rate. GDELT: run the tracked-name uniqueness scoring, sample-query BigQuery for a week of matches, measure collision rate. Confirms the gates before anything writes.
2. **YouTube adapter** first: full-history backfill of roster channels, then incremental; nearest to the proven podcast mechanics, immediate crossover with entry point #3.
3. **Conference adapter**: roster v1, current year \+ Wayback backfill per event.
4. **GDELT overlay** last, behind the uniqueness gate and count floor, shipping as intelligence-layer evidence only per the decision above; scoring promotion is a separate later call.

## Cost estimate (order of magnitude, validate in Phase 0)

YouTube Data API is \$0 inside the 10K-unit/day quota: roster full-history polling costs single-digit units per channel page, and person-side searches at 100 units each get a daily budget. One-time backfill: ~15-30K roster videos through tier-1 extraction ≈ \$15-30, a few thousand adjudications ≈ \$10-25, conference bio extraction ≈ \$5-15. GDELT on BigQuery: the initial 24-month pass scans ~1-4 TiB of partitioned GKG columns ≈ \$5-25 at on-demand rates, then daily incremental partitions for pennies. Total one-time ≈ \$40-100; steady state ≈ \$10-20/mo infra, plus the same queue-gated vendor-credit caveat for net-new guests as every lane. Engineering per the rollout: a 3-4 day spike, then roughly 2-3 weeks across the three adapters.

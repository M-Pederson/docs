Every adjustable lever in the leverage-loop pipeline, in one place. Because raw comparison scores are **saved, never interpreted at storage time** ([Persona Scoring](/guides/open-work/personas/scoring)), every lever below can be moved and re-run as pure arithmetic or a cheap LLM pass — no persona rebuilds, no re-embedding, no recompute of saved cosines.

## The five core levers

| Lever | Current | Where it lives | What changing it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Total candidates cluster** | **24** (quota table by archetype count K) | [Persona Scoring · Step 2](/guides/open-work/personas/scoring) | Bigger cluster = better recall into the WHY/judge stages and a deeper hidden bench for "show more" — at linear cost in WHY generations, graph checks, and judge context. Smaller = cheaper and sharper, but one bad elimination wave can thin the bench. |
| **LLM system prompt — THE WHY** | v1 draft (copy-paste block) | [Persona Scoring · Step 4](/guides/open-work/personas/scoring) | Controls the voice and evidence discipline of every visible suggestion. The grounding rules (no invented facts, mutual value, timing over similarity) are the quality floor — loosen them and hallucinated intros reach users. |
| **Model — THE WHY** | `moonshotai/kimi-k3` | Step 4 runner (to build) | The WHY is a _grounded_ writing task — the guitar-canary constraint applies (only kimi-k3 and claude-sonnet-5 have stayed grounding-clean in our bake-offs). Cheaper creative models (glm, deepseek) are NOT safe here the way they are for simulation. |
| **LLM system prompt — JUDGE** | v1 draft (criteria: mutual value → timing → actionability → credibility → diversity) | [Persona Scoring · Step 5](/guides/open-work/personas/scoring) | Reordering the criteria reshapes the visible six — e.g. promoting _diversity_ spreads the six across archetypes; promoting _timing_ favors current\_needs-driven cases. |
| **Model — JUDGE** | `moonshotai/kimi-k3` (tunable) | Step 5 runner (to build) | Runs **once per loop** over 24 short WHYs — cheap enough that a stronger reasoning model is affordable if ranking quality proves model-sensitive. Judging is comparative, not generative, so grounding risk is low; any strong model qualifies. |

Also adjustable at the same level: the **final visible count** (6 of 24 — ranks 7–24 persist with `filtered = true` and surface on request) and the **90-day freshness windows** on personas and simulated personas.

## Scoring levers — what to adjust and what it does to the cluster

Proposed tuning surface for Step 2, ordered by how much each lever moves cluster quality:

| # | Lever | Default | Effect on the candidate cluster |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Sim-lean quota split** (per-archetype vs direct seats) | K=3: 5/5/5 ‹\+› 5 direct | The single biggest quality dial. More archetype seats = helper-signal heavy (people who can _do something_ for the target); more direct seats = peer-heavy (people _like_ the target). If users accept helper intros more than peer intros — the working hypothesis — lean harder; if peer intros win a category (e.g. founder↔founder camaraderie), give direct more seats in that mode. |
| 2 | **Quality floor `z_floor`** (per-archetype composite floor) | ~0.5 | Raising it makes archetype seats harder to earn — weak archetypes cede more seats to direct matches (the adaptive backfill), shrinking false-positive helper picks at the cost of variety. Lowering it fills quotas even from mediocre columns. Watch `below floor` counts per archetype: an archetype that never fills is a bad simulation, not a bad floor. |
| 3 | **Soft-max temperature** (driver aggregation) | 2.0 | 0 = broad similarity wins (diffusely-alike candidates); high = one strong thread wins (spike-like behavior inside the composite). Most visible on direct matches — high temperature makes the direct seats behave like mini-spikes. |
| 4 | **Spike lane size \+ thresholds** | 4 seats · raw ≥ ~0.85 or dim-z ≥ ~2.5 | More seats/looser thresholds = more single-thread wildcards (great intro hooks, higher variance); tighter = the lane only fires on genuinely extraordinary alignments and its unused seats fall back to the quota lanes. Remember: direct `current_needs` spikes are banned (same-need = competition). |
| 5 | **Corroboration bonus** (2\+ archetypes above floor) | \+0.25 z | Rewards candidates who match the helper _pattern_ rather than one archetype's phrasing. Raising it consolidates the cluster around multi-archetype matches (safer, less diverse); zeroing it treats every archetype independently (more diverse, more phrasing-sensitive). |
| 6 | **Dimension weights** in the per-source composite | loop\_generic defaults (drivers-heavy) | Per-mode shaping: a fundraising-flavored loop can weight `current_needs`×cross-cells and `domain_focus` up; a community-flavored loop can lift `passions_interests`. Cheap to A/B because everything derives from saved scores. |
| 7 | **`min_driver_dims` rankability floor** | 2 of 3 direct-diagonal drivers | Lower to 1 to admit thin-coverage personas (more candidates, noisier); raise to 3 to require fully-scored drivers (cleaner, excludes sparse people entirely). |
| 8 | **Mutuality mode** | `min` | `min` guarantees the weaker side of the intro still benefits; `harmonic` softens that guarantee; `off` maximizes one-directional fit. Moving off `min` raises scores but risks one-sided intros — the WHY stage will struggle to find the win-win. |
| 9 | **Archetype count K** (Simulated Person builder) | 1–3, model-decided on multi-need | Forcing K=1 concentrates the helper signal (and seats) on the single highest-leverage need; allowing 3 widens the search surface. Watch forced/inferred-brief builds — a guessed brief defaulting to multi-need is a guess on a guess. |

**Recommended tuning order:** move levers 1–2 first (they control the helper-vs-peer mix, which is the product thesis itself), then 3–4 (texture: broad vs spike), then 5–9 as refinements. Every change should run against the labeled-intro eval the [harness](/guides/open-work/suggestion-recipes/harness-suggestions) owns — label ~50 introductions you would and wouldn't make, grid-search these levers against acceptance, freeze, and revisit quarterly. Until that corpus exists, every default above is a starting hypothesis, not a constant.

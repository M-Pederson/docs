**Status: scope & planning only.** Nothing on this page is built. Goal: ingest the full producing block of every Broadway production from IBDB, resolve producers to `master_person` / `master_company` records, and materialize production credits as graph edges so co-producing relationships become queryable co-investment paths.

## Why

Modern Broadway shows carry 20 to 40\+ names above the title. Co-producer billing is granted at investment thresholds (typically low-to-mid six figures, varying by capitalization and era; verify in Phase 0), so the credit block functions as a public, ordered proxy for the show's cap table. The pool skews precisely toward the Orbiter ICP: finance operators, real estate principals, media executives, and family offices co-investing outside their day jobs. Each production is a shared deal; two people on the same credit block wrote checks into the same LLC. That yields person-to-person co-investment edges that PDL and Crunchbase-class sources never see, with billing tier as a check-size signal and opening/closing dates for bitemporal validity. It composes naturally with the existing film/TV credit nodes (heavy person overlap) and the Fundable investor graph.

## The key structural fact

Every Broadway production is financed as its own single-purpose entity (LLC or LP), and most raise under Reg D, so a **Form D usually exists on EDGAR for the show entity**, naming the entity and its related persons (typically the lead producers) plus offering size. The public data therefore splits two ways:

- **IBDB / Playbill credits**: the complete, ordered producing block, every co-producer included, with stable IBDB person and production IDs. Breadth.
- **SEC Form D**: government-grade corroboration of the show entity and its lead tier, plus offering amounts. Precision anchor, lead tier only.

Design accordingly: IBDB is the backbone; Form D is the corroboration and entity-unmasking layer.

<Tip>
  **The producing-shingle bridge.** A large share of credits are entities, not people: producing shingles, family LLCs, and grouped "in association with" blocks. Resolving a shingle's principals once (shingle site, Form D related persons, OpenCorporates, press) converts every credit that entity holds across shows into person-level edges. Same leverage as the family-foundation bridge in the donations plan.
</Tip>

## What the graph already has (leverage, not build)

- **IMDb & MusicBrainz waterfalls**: the queue drain contract, tiering, and promotion-audit machinery this lane should clone rather than reinvent. Born onto the unified contract from the IMDb & Music Queue Refactor, not a fourth bespoke queue.
- **Heavy person overlap with film/TV credits**: many Broadway producers already exist as enriched persons via the IMDb waterfall, so existing-graph matching starts warm.
- **Identity machinery**: `master-person-new` #13039 (kill switch #4617), `master-company-new` #12558 (kill switch #12789), Exa profile search #12936, `create-master-link` #2541, queue upsert #12854.
- **Proven agent patterns to clone**: tiered resolver `resolve_org_identity` #12984 (cheap model first, confidence-gated write-back, append-only attempts audit per #723); deep-research config `deep-person-basic` #12833 for shingle-principal research; adjudication-with-corroboration from `verify-imdb-url` #12677 v5 and `find-artist` #13168 (same-name/different-profession rejection, name-only matches capped, auto-link only at high confidence).
- **Fundable Stage-1 gating**: edges only for identities that resolve to an existing record or pass the auto-link threshold; everything else parks as `needs_review` with its evidence. Releasable later, never lost.
- **LLM Test Harness** (API group 42) for extraction and adjudication prompt evals before anything ships.

## Pipeline design: three stages

### Stage A: production discovery \+ credit extraction

Input: one season (backfill) or the current season (cron).

1. Season index sweep (IBDB, cross-checked against the League list) → production queue rows keyed by IBDB production ID.
2. Production-page fetch → deterministic HTML parse of the producing block. IBDB markup is structured; no LLM on the happy path.
3. LLM pass only for compound credit strings: splitting "A, B & C", couple credits ("John and Jane Smith"), and grouped "in association with" blocks into typed tokens. Contract below.
4. Tony sweep (annual): mark producer credits on nominated and winning productions.

Output: structured **credit mentions**, never edges directly.

| Role | Model | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compound-string splitting (step 3) | The #12984 tier-1 config (strict JSON, reasoning off) | High-volume, low-ambiguity token typing at tier-1 economics. |
| Weak-signal retry | The #12984 tier-2 config | Strings the cheap model fumbles. |
| Shingle-principal research (Stage B) | The #12833 deep-research config | The only deep-research surface in this lane. |
| Adjudication (Stage B) | The #12677 v5 config, temp 0.1 | Full-context match adjudication with corroboration guards. |

Pinned by reference so this lane inherits house model-config changes.

### Stage B: identity resolution (per mention)

Deterministic first, LLM last:

1. **IBDB person-ID match**: previously resolved IBDB IDs map straight through (the MBID / IMDb-URL anchoring pattern).
2. **Existing-graph match**: `master_person` by normalized name where a film/TV credit, a Fundable investor record, or a prior Broadway credit corroborates; existing `master_link` URL match when the mention carried one.
3. **Shingle-principal path**: entity credits → Form D related persons, OpenCorporates officers, shingle site → person candidates.
4. **Exa LinkedIn search** on the mention's identity points (co-credit overlap, era, geography, entity affiliation), #13121-style.
5. **LLM adjudication**: the #12677 v5 shape with full mention context plus candidate profile, explicit same-name/different-era-or-industry rejection. **Auto-link only at `confidence: high` / ≥0.85**; 0.6–0.85 → `needs_review`; below → park. Name-only matches are capped below the auto-link threshold regardless of model output (the #13168 guard).

Minimum to attempt auto-link: name plus **two** independent corroborating points (e.g., co-credit overlap \+ entity affiliation, or era \+ geography \+ known producing partner).

### Stage C: edge writer

MERGE `PRODUCED` with the metadata envelope; idempotent per producer-production pair. New-person policy mirrors the investor cascade: local-only leaves plus queue rows (tier TBD), kill switches respected, no recursive enrichment from a credit sweep. Closing-date updates rewrite `valid_to` only.

## Draft system prompt v0.1: credit-string splitting (not deployed)

```text
You split ONE Broadway producing-credit string into typed producer tokens.

Production: {{title}} ({{season}}). Credit line as printed: {{credit_string}}
Billing tier for this line: {{billing_tier}}

Return ONLY valid JSON: {"credits": [...]}. Each credit object:
- name: exactly as written for this token
- entity_type: "person" | "couple" | "company" | "unknown"
- persons: for couples, the individual names as written, else null
- billing_order: integer position within the line, 1-based
- association_group: true when the token sits inside an "in association with" block
- evidence_quote: the verbatim fragment for this token
- confidence: "high" | "medium" | "low"

Rules:
1. Preserve names exactly; never expand initials or normalize.
2. A couple credit ("John and Jane Smith") is one token with entity_type "couple" and both persons listed.
3. Suffixes like "Inc.", "LLC", "Productions", "Entertainment", "Group" imply entity_type "company"; a bare personal name implies "person"; ambiguous brand-like names are "unknown".
4. Do not merge tokens across lines or tiers; emit every token with its own evidence_quote.
5. If the string contains no producer tokens, return {"credits": []}.
```

The Stage-B adjudication prompt follows #12677 v5 (match yes/no/none, confidence, reasoning, same-name rejection) and gets drafted against real cases in the LLM Test Harness before deployment.

Audit-first per #723. |

## Rollout order

1. **Phase 0 spike (2-3 days)**: scrape ~20 productions across eras; validate page stability and the deterministic parse rate; measure compound-string ambiguity; sample-match ~200 credits against the existing graph to estimate warm-start overlap; confirm the co-producer billing-threshold claim; ToS review.
2. **Backfill extraction, 2000-present** (~900-1,000 productions, the co-producer-inflation era): crawler, parse, splitting, all landing in `broadway_credit_mention`. No edges yet.
3. **Shingle unmasking \+ person resolution**, prioritized by productions with the highest existing-graph overlap; edges start writing behind the Stage-1 gate.
4. **Recurring**: weekly new-openings cron, closing-date updates, annual Tony sweep. Pre-2000 backfill and Off-Broadway/West End only if Phase 0 overlap numbers justify them.

## Open questions

1. **Billing-tier weight bands**: fixed §3.6 bands per tier (proposed) vs scaling by known capitalization / Form D offering size.
2. **Materialize `CO_PRODUCED_WITH`** or derive through the production node (proposed: derive; revisit after a scoring perf test).
3. **Node label family**: new stage-production label vs extending the film/TV credit node family.
4. **Couple credits**: one edge per spouse with a shared `couple_credit` marker (proposed), or household-level treatment per the Known model.
5. **IBDB scrape posture**: pending standard review; Playbill Vault is the fallback backbone.
6. **Scope guard**: Off-Broadway, national tours, and West End are out for v1; define the overlap threshold that earns them v2.
7. **Runtime**: Xano interim vs the AlloyDB Universe backend. Same decision as the Fundable and donations lanes; the design above is runtime-agnostic.

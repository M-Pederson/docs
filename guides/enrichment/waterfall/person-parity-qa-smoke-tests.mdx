## End-to-End Pass Gate

A successful entry response is intake evidence, not the QA result. After the
waterfall settles, require all of the following:

- Three stable polls with no advancing related table, queue, job, log, or graph watermarks.
- The selected current company is fully committed at depth 0. A depth-1 queue row is not sufficient when `person_terminal_company_dependency` exists.
- The exact terminal company dependency is `success`, still points to the selected company, and is backed by company enrichment/history newer than `required_after`.
- `profile_enrichment_job`, the latest authoritative `enrich_history_person`, and `master_person.full_enrich` agree. A later failure overrides an earlier success response.
- The deep-bio job preserves the exact Cloud Run request and callback, validates the expected subject and complete `biography[]`, and has matching non-empty payload SHA-256 values in the job audit, successful `provider_payload_committed` receipt, and callback runner.
- `master_person.deep_bio` is a non-empty string array with at least 120 joined characters. Raw prose, truncated JSON, provider meta-commentary, JSON fragments, and a separate replacement job are failures, even when the entry response says success.
- Every materialized education row has both a positive school ID and a valid preserved `ATTENDED` edge UUID; neither field alone is sufficient.
- Expertise table rows and graph edges pass count, property, recency, date, tense, and description parity.

Do not call a run stuck before its band has elapsed.

| Subject shape | Example | Measured settle | Notes |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Plain person (no film/music) | Chia Jeng Yang | ~10 min | Companies + education + expertise only |
| Person + C-suite descendant | Hazel Savage | ~7 min | Descendant enriches in parallel; both reach `full_enrich` |
| Film-heavy (IMDb) | Ryan Reynolds | **14-26 min, median ~20** | n=11 runs, 2026-07-25. Spread tracks credit count |
| Music-heavy (MusicBrainz) | Chamillionaire | **14.6 min**, or **~25 min** with a selected-company retarget | n=2 clean, 2026-07-25/26. Music queues drain during the run. A retarget adds a second terminal sequence (10 receipts instead of 5) |

### How to measure it yourself

`master_person` (table **139**) carries both ends on one row — no external timing needed:

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `created_at` | `timestamp` (epoch ms, not null) | Start. Row is created **~4 s after** the seed call, since the entry point dedups and resolves identity first. Treat as ±5 s. |
| `last_full_enrich` | `timestamp` (epoch ms, **nullable**) | End. Set on completion, so it doubles as the done-flag. |

```
duration_ms = last_full_enrich - created_at
```

Use the **root** person's row only — cascaded descendants have their own, shorter
durations, and averaging across them understates the end-to-end figure.

For per-phase timing rather than one wall-clock number, `enrich_history_person`
(table **160**) carries `processing_time` alongside `created_at` / `last_updated` /
`master_person_id`.

### Why the spread is wide

Eleven Ryan Reynolds runs on 2026-07-25 came in at 13.8, 17.0, 17.8, 17.8, 19.9, 20.4,
20.9, 22.1, 24.1, 25.0 and 25.9 minutes. Two factors account for it:

1. **IMDb scrape variance.** The same subject returned **10, 38, and 54 credits** across
   runs. More credits means more titles, awards and queue writes. Queue counts are therefore
   **not comparable between runs** — compare assertions and ratios, not raw totals.
2. **Terminal closeout tail.** Entity and company work lands in the first ~3 min, entertainment
   enrichment runs as long as it needs, then Core closeout adds a **120 s quiescence window
   plus up to two task-`193` cycles (~6 min each)** — so closeout alone trails the last
   entertainment write by 2-14 min.

A receipt sitting `pending` inside that tail is expected, not stuck: the sweeper reports
`action = deferred_person_not_quiescent` with a `quiescence` block showing `still_ms` while
it waits. **Do not invoke the sweeper manually to inspect it** — its continuation writes
`master_person`, which resets the very stillness window it is waiting on.

**Sizing guidance for the Go/AlloyDB port:** budget a **30-minute** ceiling for a
film-heavy person and treat anything past 35 minutes as genuinely stuck. The floor is not
the enrichment work — it is the 120 s quiescence plus sweeper cadence, so a faster
implementation still cannot settle a run in under ~3 minutes without changing that contract.

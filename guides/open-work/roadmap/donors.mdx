## Entry points

Same invariant as the other v1.2 lanes: every route produces **donation mentions** (or resolves to filings/pages that do), and all mentions flow through shared extraction → resolution → edge-writer stages. No edge is ever written directly from a source.

| # | Entry point | Direction | Mechanism | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Org-side seeding** | Institution → gifts | `Company:School` nodes ordered by `ATTENDED` overlap → backbone lookup \+ curated-list filter \+ honor-roll sweep per institution. | P0 |
| 2 | **Foundation backbone** | Filing → gifts | Parsed grant schedules (990-PF Part XV / Schedule I) in the shared `filing_990` store → grant rows naming tracked institutions become mentions. Structured, zero LLM. | P0 |
| 3 | **Curated mega-gift lists** | List → gifts | Chronicle "Major Private Gifts", Million Dollar List, annual roundups → extraction per list snapshot, filtered to tracked institutions. | P0 |
| 4 | **Honor-roll sweeps** | Page → gifts | Exa discovery of donor honor rolls / donor walls / annual giving reports \+ Firecrawl fetch → per-page extraction LLM. | P1 |
| 5 | **Person triggers** | Person → gifts | Philanthropist/donor/trustee signals on tracked persons → targeted announced-gift search for that person. | P1 |
| 6 | **Discover misses** | Person → gifts | Discover Deep Research resolves someone as a major donor or foundation trustee → approved miss enters via the person-side lookup. | P1 |
| 7 | **New-filing piggyback** | Filing → gifts | The federal lane's rolling e-file cron lands new filings → new grant rows for tracked institutions enter as mentions. | P1 |
| 8 | **Cross-lane corroboration** | Internal | Federal-lane trustee seats and FEC employer strings upgrade pending donor resolutions; a resolved donor upgrades pending mentions elsewhere. | P2 |

## Agent build brief

Written for the coding agent building this lane. This page is self-contained; everything you need is here or explicitly linked. Do not read intent into ambiguity: if something material is undecided, follow the escalation rule at the bottom.

**Read first, in order:** [Edge Weights](/guides/ontology/edge-weights) (scale, hub rule, envelope), [Edges](/guides/ontology/edges) (conventions), [Educational Institution Donations](/guides/open-work/roadmap/educational-institution-donations) (the research record: data-source landscape, IRS disclosure rules, prompt rationale), [990s, FEC & Form D](/guides/open-work/roadmap/990-fec-form-d) (owner of your `filing_990` input), and the pattern functions cited on this page: #12984 tiered resolver, #12677 v5 adjudication, #13168 name-only cap, #12833 deep research, #723 attempts audit, Fundable Stage-1 gating, `master-company-new` #12558, `master-person-new` #13039.

**Invariants — violating any one is a failed build:**

1. One write path: source → `donation_mention` row → resolution → edge writer; no `DONOR_TO` without a `mention_ref`. This lane never fetches 990 XML — a missing filing enqueues the org to the federal lane's queue.
2. Auto-link only at ≥0.85 confidence with name plus **two** independent corroborating identity points; name-only matches are capped below the threshold no matter what the model says (#13168); 0.6–0.85 → `needs_review`; below → parked. `donor_type: anonymous` is terminal — never resolved, never guessed.
3. The trustee bridge derives, it never resolves: person-level `DONOR_TO {via_foundation_uuid}` is written **only** for trustees the federal lane has already auto-linked (their `BOARD_MEMBER_OF` edge on the foundation node exists). This lane does not re-resolve trustees.
4. New-person policy: local-only leaves \+ queue rows at the lowest tier, kill switches #4617/#12789 checked per batch, no recursive enrichment from a donation sweep — a donor's company never triggers founder or C-suite fanout here.
5. `amount_usd` only when a source states a number; vague language ("a transformative gift") never becomes a number — at extraction time and at write time.
6. Honor-roll mentions are captured with `giving_level`, but write no edges in v1 (the gift-floor default above).
7. Aggregate edge per (donor, institution): MERGE idempotent, re-running a source is a no-op; per-gift rows stay relational under the mention. Envelope (2026-07-04 standard) on every edge.
8. Fetched pages cache raw to GCS before extraction, keyed by content hash; unchanged pages skip extraction on re-runs.
9. Go on GCP only; Xano is out of bounds except disposable Phase 0 scripts that ship nothing.

**Mention-row state machine:** `unresolved → auto_linked | needs_review | rejected | anonymous`; `auto_linked → edge_written`; `needs_review → auto_linked | rejected` (human action only). Every transition appends to `donation_resolution_attempts`.

**Fixtures (Phase 0 and regression):** the Ford Foundation filings already fixtured by the federal lane (its ST-1 — the shared-store read is this lane's consumer test), one Chronicle mega-gift list snapshot, and two honor-roll pages: one with class-year notations, one with giving bands and no amounts.

**Definition of done per phase:** Phase 0 = fixture report as an appendix here (backbone yield per org, extraction precision on a 200-row audit, resolution rates by corroboration type, floor validation). Phase 1 (backbone) = foundation-level \+ derived person-level `DONOR_TO` landing behind the Stage-1 gate, consuming `filing_990` with zero fetches. Phase 2 (curated lists) = mega-gift mentions resolving through Stage B, edges landing. Phase 3 (honor-roll sweeps) = mentions captured and parked per the floor default. Each phase ends with acceptance queries green twice and crons \+ enrichment-QA checks registered.

**Acceptance queries (zero rows required):** any `DONOR_TO` missing the envelope or `mention_ref`; any person-level `DONOR_TO` from a foundation-sourced mention missing `via_foundation_uuid` or whose trustee lacks the federal lane's `BOARD_MEMBER_OF`; any edge from a mention below the auto-link gate or in `needs_review`; any edge from an `anonymous` or honor-roll mention; any person or queue row minted from an anonymous mention; any 990 fetch originating in this lane; more than one `DONOR_TO` per (donor, institution) pair; any `DONOR_TO` weight outside **{30, 33, 36, 35, 38, 40, 48}** (50 is reserved for honor-roll and unused in v1).

**Hard limit for first runs:** 25 institutions, 200 foundation filings read, 500 mentions — until acceptance queries pass twice.

**Coordination:** you consume `filing_990` (the federal lane owns it; schema changes reach you as open-question entries on that page) and own `donation_mention` \+ `donation_resolution_attempts`. `DONOR_TO` schema changes publish to the federal lane as open-question entries before you make them. ## Why

Philanthropy is a triple signal: **wealth** (capacity), **affinity** (the donor cares enough to give), and very often an **alumni tie** the graph may not yet know. `DONOR_TO` composes with `ATTENDED` for warm-path generation — "who gave to my alma mater / my target's alma mater" — and the foundation backbone resolves donors with government-grade identity data at zero vendor cost.

## The key structural fact

Donor data splits hard by **donor type**, because of IRS disclosure rules (researched 2026-07-05):

- **Individuals → public charities: NOT in IRS data.** A 501(c)(3)'s Schedule B contributor names are withheld from public inspection. There is no government database of individual gifts to universities.
- **Private foundations: fully public.** 990-PFs disclose **both** the full grant list (every grant, grantee, amount) **and** the officers/directors/trustees.

So the lane is two-track: a **free, structured foundation backbone** (990-PF grant schedules — zero LLM) plus an **agent-driven long tail** for individual donors (announced gifts, honor rolls, named buildings).

<Tip>
  **The family-foundation bridge.** Most large "individual" giving flows through family foundations — and a 990-PF lists its trustees with addresses. "The Smith Family Foundation gave \$5M to Stanford" \+ trustees John & Jane Smith = person-level `DONOR_TO` (via foundation) with government-grade identity data. Because the federal lane already resolves those trustees for its `BOARD_MEMBER_OF` edges, the person-level donation edge here is a **derivation, not a resolution** — the free tier resolves far more people than it first appears, and this lane pays nothing for it.
</Tip>

## Node & edge schemas (new in this scope)

No new node label family: foundations and corporate donors extend the existing Company family with discriminators (`foundation`, `nonprofit`), consistent with the lane-family label decision; institutions are the existing `Company:School` nodes. **One edge type is net-new** and is reused unchanged by the federal-filings lane.

### Edge: `DONOR_TO` (new): `(Person)-[:DONOR_TO]->(Company)` and `(Company)-[:DONOR_TO]->(Company)`

| Property | Type | Req | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `donor_type` | enum `person` \| `couple` \| `foundation` \| `company` | ✓ | Anonymous mentions never become edges. |
| `gift_count` | int | ✓ | Aggregate per donor–institution pair. |
| `total_usd_known` | int |  | Sum of gifts with stated amounts only (invariant 5). |
| `first_gift_year` / `last_gift_year` | int |  | From per-gift rows. |
| `purpose` | string |  | Building, scholarship, professorship, campaign — most recent or largest gift. |
| `gift_types` | enum\[\] |  | `cash` \| `pledge` \| `bequest` \| `in_kind` \| `unknown`. |
| `via_foundation_uuid` | uuid |  | Set on derived person-level edges from the trustee bridge (invariant 3). |
| `is_alumni` | bool |  | True when an `ATTENDED` edge to the same institution corroborates. |
| `weight` | float | ✓ | Derived scheme below (2026-08-25, from the [Edge Weights](/guides/ontology/edge-weights) master scale) — pending §3.6 ratification. |
| `mention_ref`, `source_urls`, `confidence` |  | ✓ | Audit \+ provenance. |
| envelope |  | ✓ | 2026-07-04 standard; deterministic description "X is a donor to Y". |

Idempotent per (donor, institution). Per-gift detail stays relational under `donation_mention`. Co-donor pairs derive through the shared institution node at score time, never materialized — and the hub rule applies hard here: universities are thousand-donor hubs.

### Weight bands (derived 2026-08-25 from the Edge Weights master scale — pending §3.6 ratification)

The 2026-08-21 cross-lane sitting did not band `DONOR_TO`; these values are derived against the [Edge Weights](/guides/ontology/edge-weights) master reference and its 2026-05-31 hub audit. The governing fact: **the recipient institution is a thousand-donor hub**, so every `DONOR_TO` sits in the Moderate band (26–40) or below — the same envelope the audit set for award/credit hub edges — and never competes with structural bonds or non-hub money edges (`INVESTED_IN` 15 targets ordinary companies; donations target hubs).

| Relationship class | Weight (lower = closer) | Anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person, direct named gift **≥\$10M** | **30** | Ceiling = `WON_AWARD` (30), the audit's strongest hub-target edge; stronger than `ATTENDED` (40) — a transformative gift signals more current affinity than having attended. |
| Person, direct named gift **≥\$1M** | **33** | Amount gradation, the house idiom (`WORKS_AT` by seniority, `AUTHORED` by recency). |
| Person, direct named gift **≥ floor (\$100K)** | **36** | Weakest named-gift tier, still above `ATTENDED`. |
| Foundation → institution (org-level) | **30**; **40** when the foundation exceeds the grant-count hub threshold | Grant-making foundations are hubs on the donor side too (Ford: thousands of grants) — bigger hub = weaker tie, the `LOCATED_IN` 70/80 precedent. Threshold set in Phase 0 (default: \>200 grants in the 4-year window). |
| Person via foundation (trustee bridge, derived) | **38** = `BOARD_MEMBER_OF` (8) \+ foundation edge (30); **48** when the foundation is hub-damped | Path-neutral shortcut: equals its canonical path, so the derived edge never beats it — the `ON_RECORD_LABEL` derived-edge principle. Bonus property: co-trustees of a family foundation sit 16 apart through the foundation node, exactly co-board strength. |
| Company (corporate donor) → institution | **35** | Corporate giving is diffuse and PR-driven — weaker affinity than a personal gift, stronger than an honor-roll listing. |
| Honor-roll-only donor | **50** (reserved; no edges in v1) | ≈ `VOLUNTEERED_AT` ended (50): a real but weak, high-volume tie. Weak band. |

Sanity checks against the master scale: two \$10M donors to the same institution sit 60 apart — the `KNOWS` default, never spuriously close; the strongest donation tie (30) out-ranks nothing in the Very-strong or Defining bands; `is_alumni` adds **no weight bonus** — the `ATTENDED` edge already exists, and multi-path composition does the work.

<Note>
  **Why not the `INVESTED_IN` band (15–25)?** Both are direct money, but the weight scale prices *hub arithmetic*, not conviction. An investment targets a small, selective node (a round has ~5–20 investors), so co-investors at 40 apart is real signal. A donation targets a thousand-donor institution — at weight 15, any two Stanford donors would sit 30 apart, closer than co-investors in the same round, which is exactly the inversion the 2026-05-31 §3.6 audit fixed for awards and cities. The sibling federal-filings lane made the same call for political money: `POLITICAL_DONOR_TO` (renamed 2026-09-05 from `CONTRIBUTED_TO`) is 60 *because committees are thousand-donor hubs*. Where donation closeness genuinely is investment-like — co-trustees of a family foundation — the model already delivers it: 8 \+ 8 = 16 apart through the foundation node, co-board strength. A v1.1 refinement may let gifts to small, low-degree nonprofits (not in v1 scope) earn toward the Strong band the way small recipients deserve.
</Note>

## Pipeline design: three stages

Go on GCP: source adapters behind the lane-family fetcher interface, raw pages cached to GCS, Pub/Sub → Cloud Run, mentions in AlloyDB, edges to FalkorDB.

### Stage A: gift discovery (per institution)

Sweeps in order of precision; output is structured **gift mentions** (contract below), never edges.

1. Foundation backbone: read grant schedules from `filing_990` for grants naming this institution — deterministic, no LLM.
2. Curated mega-gift lists filtered to this institution — one extraction per list snapshot, cached.
3. Open-web sweep: press releases, news ("\$X gift to Y"), campaign pages — web-search-grounded LLM.
4. Honor-roll / donor-wall pages: Exa discovery → Firecrawl fetch → extraction LLM per page.

| Role | Model | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open-web discovery (step 3) | `moonshotai/kimi-k2.6` \+ `openrouter:web_search`/`web_fetch`, provider-pinned, `allow_fallbacks: false` | The house deep-research config (#12833), proven for multi-source synthesis with citations. |
| Per-page extraction (steps 2, 4) | `google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite`, `reasoning: {enabled: false}`, strict JSON | #12984 tier-1 economics (~\$0.001/call); honor rolls are high-volume, low-ambiguity extraction. |
| Weak-signal retry | `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash` \+ web search | #12984 tier-2 pattern for pages the cheap model fumbles. |

### Stage B: donor identity resolution (per mention)

Deterministic first, LLM last:

1. **Existing-graph match**: `master_person` by normalized name where an `ATTENDED` edge to the same institution exists (strong prior); `master_link` URL match when the mention carried one.
2. **Trustee bridge**: foundation mentions resolve the foundation org (#12558); person-level edges then **derive** from the federal lane's resolved trustees — no Stage-B work per person (invariant 3).
3. **Exa profile search** (#12936-style) using the mention's identity points: location, title/employer, class year, spouse.
4. **LLM adjudication**: `google/gemini-3-flash-preview` (temp 0.1), full mention context \+ candidate profile — the #12677 v5 config with explicit same-name/different-person rejection. Auto-link ≥0.85 with name \+ two independent corroborations; #13168 name-only cap; 0.6–0.85 `needs_review`; below parks.

Minimum to _attempt_ auto-link: name \+ two independent corroborating identity points (e.g. class year \+ city, employer \+ spouse name).

### Stage C: edge writer

MERGE `DONOR_TO` with the envelope; idempotent per (donor, institution); derived person edges carry `via_foundation_uuid`. New-person policy mirrors the investor cascade: local-only leaves \+ lowest-tier queue rows, kill switches respected, no recursive enrichment from a donation sweep.

## Draft system prompt v0.1: gift extraction (not deployed)

Drafted on the [edu scope page](/guides/open-work/roadmap/educational-institution-donations); reproduced here as the deployable copy. Eval in the LLM Test Harness before shipping.

```text
You extract charitable gifts to ONE educational institution from the provided page content.

Institution: {{institution_name}} (also known as: {{aliases}}). Domain: {{domain}}.

Return ONLY valid JSON: {"gifts": [...]}. Each gift object:
- donor_name: exactly as written (couples: keep as written, e.g. "John and Jane Smith")
- donor_type: "person" | "couple" | "foundation" | "company" | "anonymous"
- amount_usd: number | null  (NEVER infer or convert vague language into a number)
- gift_year: number | null
- purpose: string | null  (building, scholarship, professorship, campaign name)
- gift_type: "cash" | "pledge" | "bequest" | "in_kind" | "unknown"
- giving_level: string | null  (honor-roll band when stated, e.g. "President's Circle $50,000+")
- evidence_quote: verbatim sentence(s) naming the donor and the gift
- identity_points: {location, title, employer, class_year, degree, spouse_name, other} (nulls allowed)
- confidence: "high" | "medium" | "low"

Rules:
1. Only gifts TO this institution (or its named schools/colleges/foundations). Gifts BY the institution, grants it received from government, and sponsorships are out of scope.
2. Anonymous donors: record the gift with donor_type "anonymous"; never guess identity.
3. Class-year notations ("Jane Smith '88", "MBA '02") go into identity_points.class_year/degree.
4. Honor-roll listings without amounts are valid gifts: amount_usd null + giving_level when the page states the band.
5. Do not deduplicate across pages; emit every mention with its own evidence_quote.
6. If the page contains no qualifying gifts, return {"gifts": []}.
```

The Stage-B adjudication prompt follows the #12677 v5 shape (full mention context \+ candidate profile → `match: yes/no/none`, `confidence`, reasoning; a same-name candidate in a different profession/era/geography is a **no**) — drafted against real cases in the LLM Test Harness before deployment.

## Cost estimate (order of magnitude, validate in Phase 0)

Near-zero source cost: the backbone is free and deterministic (the fetch bill lives in the federal lane), curated lists are a handful of extractions, and honor-roll extraction runs at tier-1 economics (~\$0.001/page). Backfill LLM for the top ~200 institutions (open-web discovery \+ adjudications on announced-gift mentions) ≈ \$50–150; GCP infra ≈ \$10–20/mo during backfill, under \$10/mo steady. Vendor enrichment credits apply only to net-new donor persons promoted out of the queue — lowest tier, so effectively zero until someone promotes them. Engineering: 2–3 day Phase 0 spike, then roughly 1 week for the backbone (mostly reads) and 1–2 weeks for curated \+ honor-roll stages. DonorSearch licensing is a separate decision, not in this estimate.

## Backfill

This lane deploys into a populated graph and behind the federal lane's backfill: the backbone consumes filings that lane lands, so **its 990 sweep leads, this lane follows org-by-org** (its backfill playbook already commits to completing an org's filings before this lane consumes them).

### Sweep 1 — source-side, in rollout order

**Backbone (first).**

1. Enumerate the recipient population: every School node, ordered by `ATTENDED` overlap (warm matches first, resolution-rate telemetry fast).
2. For each institution, read grant rows from `filing_990` naming it (grantee name/EIN match). Institutions whose grantor foundations have no landed filings enqueue those orgs to the federal lane and re-check on its cadence — never fetch here.
3. Write foundation-level `DONOR_TO` per grant aggregate; derive person-level edges for federal-lane-resolved trustees.
4. Checkpoint = per-(institution) status rows; a killed worker resumes from status, never from a loop cursor.

**Curated lists (second).** One-time extraction of each list snapshot (Chronicle, Million Dollar List, roundups); mentions filter to tracked institutions; full Stage-B treatment. Small-N — this sweep is hours, not days.

**Honor-roll sweeps (third).** Top-N institutions by `ATTENDED` overlap only (N from Phase 0 yield data); Exa page discovery → Firecrawl → extraction; mentions park per the floor default.

### Sweep 2 — graph-side: set-based, not per-person

Batch entry point #5 once over the existing population: persons with philanthropist/donor/trustee titles or foundation affiliations → targeted announced-gift search. Snapshot the population, record the high-water mark; later arrivals belong to the live triggers (#5/#6), not the sweep.

### The re-resolution loop

- `donation_mention` rows are durable: re-drain `unresolved` weekly during backfill, monthly steady-state; targeted retries against new-`master_person` diffs beat full re-drains.
- After each federal-lane FEC refresh, run the corroboration loop (entry #8): fresh employer strings and new trustee resolutions upgrade pending donor mentions — backfill is when the pending pile is largest.
- New trustee auto-links in the federal lane derive person-level `DONOR_TO` here without re-touching Stage B.

### Cutover to steady state

Backbone cuts over to the federal lane's rolling e-file cron (entry #7) when the institution queue drains — record per-institution high-water marks. Honor-roll pages re-sweep annually (cadence confirmed in Phase 0; most institutions publish once a year). One drain owner per queue — never run backfill and steady-state drains concurrently.

### What backfill must NOT do

- No 990 fetch from this lane, during backfill exactly as in steady state (invariant 1).
- No recursive enrichment: donor leaves are lowest-tier queue rows; kill switches #4617/#12789 checked per batch.
- No edges from honor-roll or anonymous mentions (invariants 2, 6).
- Hard caps first: 25 institutions / 200 filings / 500 mentions until acceptance queries pass twice (agent brief).

## Rollout order

1. **Phase 0 spike (2–3 days)**: read the federal lane's fixture filings through the consumer path; verify SOURCE portal export/API availability; snapshot one curated list and measure extraction precision on a 200-row audit; sweep two honor-roll fixture pages; validate the gift-floor default against real yield.
2. **Foundation backbone** — highest precision, zero LLM, and the trustee bridge resolves people for free. Gated on the federal lane's 990 adapter having landed filings.
3. **Curated mega-gift lists** — hundreds of named donors at the top of the wealth pyramid.
4. **Honor-roll sweeps** for the top-`ATTENDED` institutions — the volume tier, mention-only in v1.
5. **DonorSearch evaluation** only if 2–4 leave a coverage gap worth paying for.

Gating mirrors the Fundable Stage-1 rule: edges only for donors who resolve to an existing record or pass the auto-link threshold; everything else parks as `needs_review` with its evidence — releasable later, never lost.

## Open questions

1. **Weight-band ratification** — the bands above were derived 2026-08-25 against the [Edge Weights](/guides/ontology/edge-weights) master scale (hub rule, awards-audit envelope, derived-shortcut principle); a §3.6 sitting ratifies them plus the two Phase-0 parameters: the foundation grant-count hub threshold (default \>200 grants in the window) and whether small, low-degree nonprofit recipients (post-v1) earn toward the Strong band.
2. **Gift floor** — ratify the defaults: backbone all, announced ≥\$100K, honor-roll mention-only. The alternative cuts (announced ≥\$1M only, or honor-roll edges at weight 50) change Phase 2/3 scope, not the schema.
3. **Honor-roll edges** — if ratified later, the reserved weight-50 band releases the parked mentions without re-extraction.
4. **Commercial licensing appetite** — request a DonorSearch quote now, or after the free tiers land?
5. **International scope** — UK (Charity Commission accounts), Canada (T3010) have analogous public filings; v2.

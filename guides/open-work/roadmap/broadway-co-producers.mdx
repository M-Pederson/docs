## Entry points

**Invariants — violating any one is a failed build:**

1. One write path: every edge flows extraction → `stage_credit_mention` row → resolution → edge writer. No `PRODUCED` edge without a `mention_ref`. Person-side entry points enqueue productions; they never write credits directly.
2. Weights exact per the band table: `PRODUCED` ∈ {12, 16, 22, 28, 35} by tier. Integer scale, floor 1, never sub-1. The 2026-07-04 metadata envelope on every node and edge.
3. Auto-link only at ≥0.85 with name plus two independent corroborations; name-only matches capped below auto-link regardless of model output; everything else parks `needs_review` with evidence.
4. New-person policy: local-only leaves \+ queue rows, kill switches #4617/#12789 respected, no recursive enrichment triggered from this lane.
5. Co-production derives through the shared production node at query time. Never materialize `CO_PRODUCED_WITH`.
6. Idempotency: production nodes on `source_production_id`; `PRODUCED` on (producer, production). Re-runs on unchanged input are no-ops; closing-date updates rewrite `valid_to` only.
7. Raw HTML cached to GCS before parsing; retries parse from cache, never re-fetch. Throttle ≤1 rps per source until the Phase 0 ToS memo lands; no bulk crawl before it does.
8. Go on GCP only. Xano is out of bounds except disposable Phase 0 scripts that ship nothing.

**Mention-row state machine:** `unresolved → auto_linked | needs_review | rejected`; `auto_linked → edge_written`; `needs_review → auto_linked | rejected` (human action only). No other transitions; every transition appends to `stage_credit_resolution_attempts`.

**Fixtures (Phase 0 and regression):** The Outsiders (`ibdb:537824`, the worked example above: couples, slash credit, shingles, theatre-owner double role), one pre-2010 production for format drift, one revival, one current West End Broadway transfer, one grouped "in association with" block.

## Why

Modern Broadway shows carry 20 to 40\+ names above the title. Co-producer billing is granted at investment thresholds (typically low-to-mid six figures, varying by capitalization and era; verify in Phase 0), so the credit block functions as a public, ordered proxy for the show's cap table. The pool skews precisely toward the Orbiter ICP: finance operators, real estate principals, media executives, and family offices co-investing outside their day jobs. Each production is a shared deal; two people on the same credit block wrote checks into the same LLC. That yields person-to-person co-investment edges that PDL and Crunchbase-class sources never see, with billing tier as a check-size signal and opening/closing dates for bitemporal validity. It composes naturally with the existing film/TV credit nodes (heavy person overlap) and the Fundable investor graph.

The West End is the same investor class with heavily overlapping producing teams (Broadway transfers share lead producers and shingles), so including it in v1 adds transatlantic co-investment edges for the cost of one extra source adapter.

## The key structural fact

Every Broadway production is financed as its own single-purpose entity (LLC or LP), and most raise under Reg D, so a **Form D usually exists on EDGAR for the show entity**, naming the entity and its related persons (typically the lead producers) plus offering size. The public data therefore splits two ways:

- **IBDB / Playbill credits**: the complete, ordered producing block, every co-producer included, with stable IBDB person and production IDs. Breadth.
- **SEC Form D**: government-grade corroboration of the show entity and its lead tier, plus offering amounts. Precision anchor, lead tier only.

The UK inverts this. West End productions are financed through UK entities, and **Companies House discloses officers and persons with significant control for free via API**: a stronger unmasking layer than Form D (full officer lists, not lead-tier related persons). The UK gap is on the credits side instead: no IBDB equivalent with stable IDs, so credit sourcing leans on SOLT listings, Theatricalia, programmes, and press, with coverage depth to validate in Phase 0.

Design accordingly: credits backbone per market (IBDB / SOLT\+Theatricalia); entity corroboration per market (Form D / Companies House).

<Tip>
  **The producing-shingle bridge.** A large share of credits are entities, not people: producing shingles, family LLCs, and grouped "in association with" blocks. Resolving a shingle's principals once (shingle site, Form D related persons, Companies House officers, OpenCorporates, press) converts every credit that entity holds across shows and both markets into person-level edges. Same leverage as the family-foundation bridge in the donations plan.
</Tip>

## Pipeline design: three stages

Go on GCP end to end: per-market source adapters behind one fetcher interface, raw HTML cached to GCS, Pub/Sub → Cloud Run workers, mentions and attempts in AlloyDB, edges to FalkorDB.

### Stage A: production discovery \+ credit extraction

Input: one season per market (backfill) or the current season (cron).

1. Season index sweep per market (IBDB cross-checked against the League list; SOLT listings cross-checked against Theatricalia) → production queue rows keyed by the market anchor.
2. Production-page fetch through the source adapter → deterministic HTML parse of the producing block. No LLM on the happy path.
3. LLM pass only for compound credit strings: splitting "A, B & C", couple credits ("John and Jane Smith"), and grouped "in association with" blocks into typed tokens. Contract below.
4. Awards sweep (annual): mark producer credits on Tony and Olivier nominated/winning productions.

Output: structured **credit mentions**, never edges directly.

| Role | Model | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compound-string splitting (step 3) | The #12984 tier-1 config (strict JSON, reasoning off) | High-volume, low-ambiguity token typing at tier-1 economics. |
| Weak-signal retry | The #12984 tier-2 config | Strings the cheap model fumbles. |
| Shingle-principal research (Stage B) | The #12833 deep-research config | The only deep-research surface in this lane. |
| Adjudication (Stage B) | The #12677 v5 config, temp 0.1 | Full-context match adjudication with corroboration guards. |

Pinned by reference so this lane inherits house model-config changes.

### Stage B: identity resolution (per mention)

Deterministic first, LLM last:

1. **Source-ID match**: previously resolved IBDB (and, if Phase 0 validates, Theatricalia) IDs map straight through, the MBID / IMDb-URL anchoring pattern.
2. **Existing-graph match**: `master_person` by normalized name where a film/TV credit, a Fundable investor record, or a prior stage credit corroborates; existing `master_link` URL match when the mention carried one.
3. **Shingle-principal path**: entity credits → Form D related persons (US) / Companies House officers and PSCs (UK) / OpenCorporates / shingle site → person candidates.
4. **Exa LinkedIn search** on the mention's identity points (co-credit overlap, era, geography, entity affiliation), #13121-style.
5. **LLM adjudication**: the #12677 v5 shape with full mention context plus candidate profile, explicit same-name/different-era-or-industry rejection. **Auto-link only at `confidence: high` / ≥0.85**; 0.6–0.85 → `needs_review`; below → park. Name-only matches are capped below the auto-link threshold regardless of model output (the #13168 guard).

Minimum to attempt auto-link: name plus **two** independent corroborating points (e.g., co-credit overlap \+ entity affiliation, or era \+ geography \+ known producing partner).

### Stage C: edge writer

MERGE `PRODUCED` with the metadata envelope; idempotent per producer-production pair. New-person policy mirrors the investor cascade: local-only leaves plus queue rows (tier TBD), kill switches respected, no recursive enrichment from a credit sweep. Closing-date updates rewrite `valid_to` only.

## Draft system prompt v0.1: credit-string splitting (not deployed)

```text
You split ONE stage-production producing-credit string into typed producer tokens.

Production: {{title}} ({{season}}, {{market}}). Credit line as printed: {{credit_string}}
Billing tier for this line: {{billing_tier}}

Return ONLY valid JSON: {"credits": [...]}. Each credit object:
- name: exactly as written for this token
- entity_type: "person" | "couple" | "company" | "unknown"
- persons: for couples, the individual names as written, else null
- billing_order: integer position within the line, 1-based
- association_group: true when the token sits inside an "in association with" block
- evidence_quote: the verbatim fragment for this token
- confidence: "high" | "medium" | "low"

Rules:
1. Preserve names exactly; never expand initials or normalize.
2. A couple credit ("John and Jane Smith") is one token with entity_type "couple" and both persons listed.
3. Suffixes like "Inc.", "LLC", "Ltd", "Productions", "Entertainment", "Group" imply entity_type "company"; a bare personal name implies "person"; ambiguous brand-like names are "unknown".
4. Do not merge tokens across lines or tiers; emit every token with its own evidence_quote.
5. If the string contains no producer tokens, return {"credits": []}.
```

The Stage-B adjudication prompt follows #12677 v5 (match yes/no/none, confidence, reasoning, same-name rejection) and gets drafted against real cases in the LLM Test Harness before deployment.

## Worked example: The Outsiders (simulated 2026-08-21)

Dry run against public data; nothing written. Subject: the 2024 Tony Best Musical at the Bernard B. Jacobs Theatre, opened 2024-04-11. Five lead-producer credits (The Araca Group, American Zoetrope, Olympus Theatricals, Sue Gilad & Larry Rogowsky, Angelina Jolie) and 35\+ co-producer credits: the modern credit-block shape this lane exists for.

**Entry points fired:** #5 season backfill (2023-24 season, P1), #7 awards sweep (`award_winner: true`), #3 producer-crossover for the film-credit holders in the block, and #8 shingle expansion fanning out from Araca, Shubert, Nederlander, and John Gore across their other productions.

**Extraction notes:** the block is a token-typing gauntlet. Couples split into paired person edges (Sue Gilad & Larry Rogowsky in the lead tier; Jonathan & Michelle Clay, Jennifer & Jonathan Allan Soros, LaChanze & Marylee Fairbanks in the co-producer tier). The slash credit "Geffen Playhouse/Howard Tenenbaum/Linda B. Rubin" splits to one org \+ two persons in an association group. The Shubert Organization appears as both venue owner and co-producer; only the credit yields an edge. Entity credits span film (American Zoetrope), music (Sony Music Masterworks), studio (Warner Bros. Theatre Ventures), and pooled-investor vehicles (The Broadway Investor's Club).

## Cost estimate (order of magnitude, validate in Phase 0)

Sources are \$0: IBDB/Playbill/SOLT scraping, EDGAR, and Companies House, with OpenCorporates freemium as needed. One-time backfill across ~1,800-2,000 productions at #12984 tier economics: ~6-10K splitting calls ≈ \$10-20, ~4-6K Stage-B adjudications ≈ \$25-60, ~400-700 shingle deep-research runs ≈ \$20-100, so total one-time LLM ≈ \$60-180. GCP (Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, GCS cache, AlloyDB increment) ≈ \$20-40/mo during backfill, under \$10/mo steady at ~70-80 new productions/yr. The only line that scales into real money is vendor enrichment credits (PDL / Enrich Layer per-match) for net-new persons promoted out of the queue, and that spend is already gated by queue tier. The dominant cost is engineering time per the rollout: a 3-4 day spike, then roughly 2-3 weeks to recurring.

## Backfill

This lane deploys into a **populated graph**: by the time the first `PRODUCED` edge writes, the IMDb, music, Fundable, and podcast lanes will have created hundreds of thousands of `master_person` / `master_company` rows, and daily enrichment keeps adding more underneath you. Backfill is therefore not "load the sources and go" — it is two sequenced sweeps plus a standing re-resolution loop that keeps paying out as the graph grows. This section operationalizes rollout phases 2–3.

### Sweep 1 — source-side: seed every production first

1. Seed once per market from the season indexes (Broadway 2000-present, ~900-1,000 productions; West End window per Phase 0) into the unified queue in the P1/P2 bands of entry point #5. A year is the seeding unit; seeding is an upsert on `source_production_id`, so re-seeding a season is a no-op.
2. Drain through **Stage A only** at first: every production lands its `stage_credit_mention` rows with zero edges written. Mentions are durable; edges are releasable later, never lost (the Stage-1 gate).
3. Go mechanics: one seeding job per market; a Pub/Sub topic per stage; Cloud Run workers with concurrency tuned to the ≤1 rps **per-source** throttle — parallelism scales across sources, never across pages of one source. Raw HTML lands in GCS before parse; a retried production parses from cache and must produce byte-identical mention rows (assert this in Phase 0).
4. Checkpointing: the queue row **is** the checkpoint. Status transitions (`queued → fetched → parsed → mentions_landed`) mean a killed worker resumes by re-reading status — never by an in-memory cursor or loop position.
5. Hard cap: first 50 productions, acceptance queries green twice, then open the bands (per the agent brief).

### Sweep 2 — graph-side: run the existing population through the person-side entry points

Entry points #1–#3 are written as triggers, but at deploy time each must run **once as a batch sweep** over the people already in the graph:

| Sweep | Population | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Producer-crossover (#3) | Every `master_person` holding a film/TV credit with a producing role | Highest prior for stage crossover. Enumerate from existing credit edges; IBDB person-page lookup; hits enqueue that person's productions. |
| Expertise-domain (#1) | Every person carrying a theatre/performing-arts domain from the 70-domain taxonomy | Phase 0 fixes the qualifying-domain list; sweep once, then the trigger owns new derivations. |
| Enrichment-signal (#2) | Persons whose stored enrichment shows producer-shaped signals (titles, shingle employers, Broadway/West End bio mentions) | One-time scan of stored enrichment output; then the waterfall trigger owns it. |

- Run sweep 2 **after** sweep 1's mention landing has begun: person-page lookups mostly enqueue productions the season backfill already covered, so a hit costs one lookup, not a pipeline run. Dedup is free — enqueue upserts on the production anchor.
- Read the person population as a snapshot and record the high-water mark (`master_person.id` or `created_at`). Persons created after the mark belong to the live trigger path, not the sweep — re-running the sweep must not chase a moving population.

### The re-resolution loop (the part that is easy to miss)

Stage-B resolution quality is a function of graph density **at resolution time**. A co-producer who is unresolvable in week 1 may be trivial in week 6 because the Fundable lane added them, the media lane resolved them as a podcast guest, or the filings lane landed their Form D. The mention store is durable precisely so resolution can be retried:

- Re-drain `unresolved` mentions on a schedule — weekly during backfill, monthly steady-state — cheapest matchers first: a source-ID or existing-graph match on retry costs nothing.
- Targeted retries beat full re-drains: diff new `master_person` rows since the last pass (high-water mark) and re-attempt only mentions whose normalized name matches a new arrival.
- Every retry appends to `stage_credit_resolution_attempts`; never overwrite a prior attempt — the attempts log is how resolution-rate drift gets detected.
- `needs_review` rows are excluded from automated retries: those wait for human action, per the state machine.

### Cutover to steady state

Backfill is done per market when the season queue has drained through the bands and the weekly re-resolution retry yield falls below the Phase-0-set threshold. Record the cutover date. After it, the weekly new-openings cron and annual award sweeps own everything — never leave a backfill drain running beside the steady-state cron (one drain owner per queue).

### What backfill must NOT do

- Never trigger recursive enrichment: unresolved credits create local-only leaves \+ queue rows, tier-gated; the enrichment lanes decide when vendor credits get spent.
- Never write edges during the mention-landing phase; the Stage-1 gate opens per rollout phase 3.
- Never re-fetch on retry — parse from the GCS cache.
- Check kill switches #4617/#12789 **per batch**, not per run: a switch flipped mid-backfill must stop the next batch, not the next backfill.

## Rollout order

1. **Phase 0 spike (3-4 days)**: US: scrape ~20 productions across eras, validate page stability and the deterministic parse rate, measure compound-string ambiguity, sample-match ~200 credits against the existing graph for warm-start overlap, confirm the co-producer billing-threshold claim. UK: validate SOLT/Theatricalia producer-credit coverage and ID stability on ~10 productions, run the Companies House officer join on ~5 show entities, pick the UK anchor. ToS review for both markets.
2. **Backfill**: seed the production queue per market from season indexes (Broadway 2000-present, ~900-1,000 productions, the co-producer-inflation era; West End window per Phase 0, 2000-present proposed), then drain in the priority bands defined under Entry points. All landing in `stage_credit_mention`; no edges yet.
3. **Shingle unmasking \+ person resolution**, prioritized by productions with the highest existing-graph overlap; edges start writing behind the Stage-1 gate.
4. **Recurring**: weekly new-openings cron per market, closing-date updates, annual Tony \+ Olivier sweeps.
